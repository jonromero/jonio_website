## Why NBA 
NBA franchise values are up 10x+ in the last 15 years. Not because basketball got 10x better. Because:

1/ Scarcity. 30 teams. Supply doesn't grow. The waiting list of people who want to own an NBA team is not getting shorter.

2/ Media. The league just signed a $76B, 11-year deal with Disney, NBC, and Amazon. Year 1 is THIS season. That money flows into every franchise's revenue base now, contractually.

3/ Global demand. The NBA is the only US league with genuine international premium. The product exports in a way the NFL doesn't (hint: NBA Europe)

4/ The expansion fee wildcard. Seattle and Las Vegas expansion teams will trigger fees distributed to all 30 existing franchises. 

5/ WNBA is growing fast and the Sirens are the new team in Cleveland.
